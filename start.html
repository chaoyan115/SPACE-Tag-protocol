SPACE-Tag Protocols

Spatial CUT&Tag chromatin profiling on 10x Genomics Visium and Visium HD. An antibody against a histone mark recruits protein A-Tn5, which tagments chromatin and inserts a T7 promoter. In vitro transcription turns the tagmented fragments into polyadenylated RNA, which is captured by the spatially barcoded oligonucleotides of the slide.

The protocols

Page Use it for
CUT&Tag test on tissue sections Checking that an antibody directs tagmentation in a new tissue, with standard adapters and a gel readout
IVT test on plain slides Checking the full Day 1 chemistry, including clearing, gap filling and IVT, before using a spatial slide
SPACE-Tag (Visium) Spatial chromatin profiling on standard Visium slides, 55 µm spots
SPACE-Tag-HD (Visium HD) Spatial chromatin profiling on Visium HD with the CytAssist, 2 µm barcoded squares

Day 1 (antibodies, tagmentation, clearing, gap filling, IVT) is the same in all three. They differ in where the IVT RNA goes next.

How to start

 1. Section and check the tissue
    Fresh-frozen, 10 µm sections. H&E on one section to confirm morphology.
        │
 2. Validate the antibody on a plain slide
    Immunofluorescence on the same tissue, same fixation and permeabilization.
        │
 3. CUT&Tag test on a plain slide                   → CUT&Tag test page
    Day 1 up to tagmentation with standard Nextera adapters, then PCR and a gel.
    Go on when antibody wells clearly beat the no-antibody control.
        │
 4. IVT test on a plain slide                       → IVT test page
    Day 1 as written, then RNA purification, Qubit and TapeStation.
    Go on when the reference wells clearly beat the no-T7 and no-antibody controls.
        │
 5. Spatial experiment                              → SPACE-Tag or SPACE-Tag-HD page
    Regular Visium, or Visium HD on the CytAssist.
        │
 6. Library QC
    Fragment analyzer trace, then a shallow sequencing run: duplication rate,
    fraction of reads in peaks, valid spatial barcodes, fragments per spot or bin.
        │
 7. Deep sequencing and analysis

Steps 2 to 4 cost a few glass slides, a gel and one TapeStation run. They catch most failures (a weak antibody, an inactive PA-Tn5 batch, a tissue that needs a different clearing time) before a spatial slide is used.

Citation and feedback

If you use SPACE-Tag, please cite:

Yan C., He R., Fernandez S., Pleska M., Maniatis S., Raimondi I., Vickovic S. SPACE-Tag enables high-throughput spatial chromatin profiling with CUT&Tag. bioRxiv (2026). doi:10.64898/2026.11.06.764689

The preprint is under peer review, and this protocol may be updated as the paper is revised.

Questions and reports of what did or did not work in your hands are welcome as issues.
