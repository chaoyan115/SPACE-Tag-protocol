CUT&Tag Test on Tissue Sections

The first bench check for a new tissue or antibody. Tissue sections on ordinary glass slides go through the antibody and tagmentation steps of SPACE-Tag Day 1, but the PA-Tn5 carries standard Nextera adapters instead of the SPACE-Tag T7 adapters. The tagmented DNA is released with proteinase K, amplified by PCR and run on a gel. It answers one question cheaply: does this antibody direct tagmentation in this tissue?

When to run it

  • A new tissue type.
  • A new antibody or antibody lot.
  • A new PA-Tn5 batch.

Run it after the antibody has been checked by immunofluorescence and before the IVT test. It does not test the SPACE-Tag-specific steps (tissue clearing, gap filling, IVT), which is what the IVT test is for.

Design

16-well gasket (ArrayIT AHC1X16 or Grace Biolabs ProPlate 244864) on a standard glass slide, 10 µm sections, one section per well.

Well Purpose Expected result
Antibody of interest, in duplicate Targeted tagmentation PCR product clearly above the no-antibody well
No primary antibody Untargeted PA-Tn5 background Little or no product
No PA-Tn5 (optional) Contaminating DNA and primer artefacts No product
Positive-control antibody (optional), e.g. a well-characterized H3K27me3 or H3K4me3 antibody Shows the tissue and PA-Tn5 work Strong product

Transposome with standard Nextera adapters

Load PA-Tn5 exactly as in Day 0 and Step 0 of the SPACE-Tag protocol, replacing the two SPACE-Tag adapters with the standard Tn5 mosaic-end adapters (Picelli et al. 2014). Anneal each of Tn5ME-A and Tn5ME-B with Tn5MErev.

Oligo Sequence (5′→3′)
Tn5ME-A TCGTCGGCAGCGTCAGATGTGTATAAGAGACAG
Tn5ME-B GTCTCGTGGGCTCGGAGATGTGTATAAGAGACAG
Tn5MErev /5Phos/CTGTCTCTTATACACATCT

Day 1: antibodies and tagmentation

Follow the SPACE-Tag protocol, Steps 1 to 5 (fixation, permeabilization, primary antibody, secondary antibody, PA-Tn5 binding and tagmentation), using the Nextera-loaded PA-Tn5. Stop after tagmentation: skip tissue clearing, gap filling and IVT.

Release the DNA with proteinase K

  1. Remove the tagmentation buffer.
  2. Per well, prepare 9 µl proteinase K (NEB P8107S) + 61 µl Buffer PKD (Qiagen 1034963) and add the 70 µl onto the tissue.
  3. Seal the wells and incubate 1 h at 50 °C.
  4. Transfer the digest of each well to a labelled 1.5 ml tube.

Purify the DNA

Purify each digest with the Zymo ChIP DNA Clean & Concentrator (D5201), or with 2× AMPure XP beads. Elute in 50 µl.

PCR

Gap filling. Mix the 50 µl eluate with 50 µl OneTaq 2× Master Mix (NEB M0482) and incubate 5 min at 68 °C in a thermal cycler. Tn5 leaves a 9 nt gap at each insertion; this step fills it so that the fragments can be amplified, and it must come before any denaturation.

Amplification. To the same tube, add:

Component Volume (µl)
Gap-filled reaction (from above) 100
i5 primer (S50x), 10 µM 7.5
i7 primer (N70x), 10 µM 7.5
OneTaq 2× Master Mix 25
Nuclease-free water 10
Total 150

Each primer ends at 0.5 µM and the master mix at 1×. Cycle as in the OneTaq 2× Master Mix protocol (NEB M0482), with a 55 °C anneal and a 1 min extension:

Step Temperature Time Cycles
Initial denaturation 94 °C 30 s 1
Denaturation 94 °C 30 s 12
Annealing 55 °C 30 s 12
Extension 68 °C 1 min 12
Final extension 68 °C 5 min 1
Hold 4 °C

The best cycle number depends on the tissue and the antibody. Adjust it if the product is faint or the gel shows over-amplification.

Primers (5′→3′; [i5] and [i7] are 8 nt index sequences):

Primer Sequence
i5 (S50x) AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACAC[i5]TCGTCGGCAGCGTC
i7 (N70x) CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT[i7]GTCTCGTGGGCTCGG

Clean-up. Double-sided AMPure XP size selection. Add 0.45× beads and keep the supernatant, which removes large fragments. Add beads to the supernatant up to 1×, keep the beads, wash and elute.

Readout

Run the library on a 2% agarose gel next to a 100 bp ladder, or on a TapeStation (High Sensitivity D5000 ScreenTape).

  • Antibody wells show clearly more product than the no-antibody well.
  • The no-PA-Tn5 well is empty apart from primer dimer.
  • Duplicates look alike.

Example gel (2% agarose). Two conventional H3K27ac CUT&Tag libraries from tissue sections, tagmented under the conditions used for SPACE-Tag. The libraries show mono-, di- and tri-nucleosomal fragments; the PCR primers add 99 bp to each fragment, so the mononucleosome band runs near 300 bp. The bands at the bottom are primer dimer, which the double-sided clean-up removes. M, E-Gel 1 Kb Plus DNA Ladder.

If the antibody wells look no better than the no-antibody well, change the antibody or its dilution before moving on to the IVT test. Libraries that pass can also be sequenced shallowly to check enrichment at known loci.
